Universal Statement Rules

1. Avoid Unqualified Absolutes: Never claim "100% ready", "flawless", "zero defect", or "certified" unless citing a specific historical benchmark qualified in text.

2. Preserve Simulation Truth: Distinguish internal simulation (PaperExecutionAdapter, MockMarketDataProvider) from external integrations (TwelveData, OandaBrokerAdapter).

3. No Financial Guarantees: Past backtest or optimization results must never be represented as live market performance guarantees.

4. Clean Transaction Boundaries: Describe the asset as a proposed software and intellectual property transfer, subject to executed agreements.
