1. Executive Transaction Scope Overview

The proposed transaction is structured as a software and intellectual property asset acquisition, subject to executed definitive transaction agreements. Under the proposed transferable software/IP scope, the acquiring entity would receive proprietary source codebases, quantitative algorithms, automated test harnesses, database schemas, and closing due diligence dossiers. To eliminate third-party account transfer friction and ensure clean corporate separation, cloud hosting instances, payment processor accounts, and external market data keys are independently provisioned by the buyer under their corporate entity.
