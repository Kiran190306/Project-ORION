### Internal Implementation Notice
> This document establishes mandatory technical and operational risk disclosures for users interacting with Project ORION's quantitative simulation and paper trading facilities. It must be prominently acknowledged prior to platform usage.

---

### 1. Simulated Paper Trading Only ($0.00 Real Capital at Risk)
All trading activities, strategy evaluations, order submissions, and portfolio metrics available on Project ORION operate exclusively in **Paper Trading Simulation Mode**.
- Virtual Capital: Accounts are provisioned with virtual demo balances (e.g. $100,000.00 USD virtual currency).
- Zero Financial Risk: Real capital is **never** at risk on the Platform ($0.00 capital at risk). The Platform does not maintain client funds, escrow, custody, or deposit accounts.
- No Real Money Execution: Orders submitted through the interface or API are routed directly to an internal software simulation matching engine and never to an active interbank market, liquidity pool, or live broker execution desk.

### 2. Execution Differences & Simulation Limitations
Simulated paper trading inherently differs from live market execution in several critical ways:
- **Liquidity & Depth:** In paper simulation, orders are assumed to be filled against model prices without affecting market depth. In live trading, large orders cause market impact and price movement.
- **Slippage & Spread:** While ORION incorporates mathematical Gaussian models for adverse slippage and spread widening, actual market volatility, news events, and liquidity drying up may result in substantially worse execution in live trading.
- **Latency & Queue Priority:** Simulated fills do not account for physical exchange distance, order book queue priority, or broker order-routing delays.
- **Partial Fills & Rejections:** Real market orders may experience rejections, partial fills, or cancellations that differ from deterministic algorithmic models.

### 3. Hypothetical & Backtested Performance Warning
Hypothetical, simulated, or backtested performance results have inherent limitations:
- **No Financial Track Record:** Backtested results do not represent actual trading and cannot account for real financial risk.
- **Hindsight & Optimization Bias:** Algorithmic strategies developed using historical data may be subject to over-fitting, survivorship bias, or parameter curve-fitting, despite walk-forward validation controls.
- **No Guarantee of Future Profitability:** A Quality Gate PASS or high historical Sharpe/Sortino ratio indicates historical mathematical consistency under test conditions only. It **MUST NEVER** be interpreted as guaranteed or expected live profitability.

### 4. Software Only — No Investment Advice or Fiduciary Duty
- Project ORION is a software technology platform, not a registered broker-dealer, investment adviser, commodity trading advisor (CTA), or financial institution.
- The Platform does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.
- No content, tearsheet, automated metric, or score generated by the Software constitutes a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any currency pair, security, or financial instrument.
- Use of the Platform does not establish an advisory, broker, or fiduciary relationship between you and the Platform developers.

### 5. Broker Sandbox Integration Disclaimer
The Platform supports connectivity to external broker practice environments (such as the OANDA v20 practice sandbox). These connections are for sandbox integration testing and paper reconciliation only. Practice broker environments do not reflect real market fills, execution speed, or credit risk.
