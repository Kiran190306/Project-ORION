# Refund & Subscription Cancellation Policy

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-22
**Status:** Draft for legal review — requires owner & qualified legal counsel review prior to commercial launch.

---

### Internal Implementation Notice
> This document describes the current operational mechanics of subscription billing and cancellation in Project ORION. Commercial refund rules and statutory consumer rights (such as EU statutory withdrawal periods) require formal owner approval and external legal counsel review before commercial launch.

---

### 1. Beta Testing Environment (Stripe Test Mode)
During the Public Beta phase of Project ORION:
- All commercial billing checkouts, upgrades, and subscription plans operate strictly through **Stripe Test Mode** (`sk_test_...`).
- Test payment card numbers provided by Stripe are utilized for sandbox verification.
- **Zero real currency is billed, charged, or debited** to beta users. Consequently, financial refunds are not applicable during test mode operations.

### 2. Commercial Subscription Lifecycle & Auto-Renewal
Upon official commercial activation:
- Subscriptions to paid tiers (e.g. `PRO`, `BUSINESS`, `ENTERPRISE`) are billed on a recurring monthly billing cycle.
- Unless cancelled prior to the renewal date, subscriptions renew automatically at the beginning of each billing period.

### 3. Self-Service Subscription Cancellation
- **How to Cancel:** Organization Owners and Admins with `SUBSCRIPTION_MANAGE` authority may initiate subscription cancellation at any time directly through the **Commercial Billing & Plans** section of the dashboard.
- **Cancellation at Period End (Default):** When you cancel, your paid subscription tier and associated quotas (daily order quotas, accounts, worker allocations) remain active until the conclusion of the current prepaid billing period.
- **Degradation to Free Sandbox:** Following the end of the billing period, your organization automatically degrades to the default Free Sandbox tier limits. Your historical trading logs, positions, and accounts are preserved and not deleted.

### 4. Failed Payments & Grace Handling
In the event that an automated recurring payment fails:
- The system attempts automated retry logic via the billing processor.
- If payment cannot be completed, subscription status transitions to unpaid/past_due and entitlements degrade fail-closed to Free Sandbox limits to prevent unauthorized resource consumption.

### 5. Refund Discretion & Statutory Rights
- **Current Operational Baseline:** SaaS digital software access fees are treated as earned upon account provisioning. Refunds for partial billing months are not automatically generated by the platform billing system.
- **Subject to Policy Approval:** Final commercial refund eligibility, exceptional circumstance reviews, and cooling-off periods are subject to formal owner approval and statutory consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction *(requires jurisdiction-specific legal review)*.
- **Billing Dispute Inquiries:** To report billing discrepancies or request administrative subscription adjustments, contact platform support through the designated administrative channels.
