║                  SECURITY NOTES                          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  - Secrets are NOT included in this chart.
  - Use external-secrets, SealedSecrets, or SOPS.
  - TLS is terminated at the ingress.
  - All pods run as non-root with read-only filesystems.
  - NetworkPolicies enforce micro-segmentation.
